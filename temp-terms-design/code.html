Agreement Overview

These terms explain how Relux Hub works and how users can safely use our clothing resale and rental platform. We aim to foster a community of sustainable fashion enthusiasts built on trust.

shopping_bag

1. Platform Usage

Relux Hub is a curated marketplace for clothes. Users can buy, sell, and rent pre-loved clothing items. We facilitate a circular economy and strictly prohibit any misuse of the platform, including fraudulent listings or automated bot activity.

sell

2. Seller Responsibilities

As a seller, you agree to provide 100% accurate information regarding item condition, sizing, and brand. Only genuine clothing is allowed. You are legally responsible for the items you list and must ensure they match the description provided.

person_check

3. Buyer Responsibilities

Buyers must read all product details before purchasing or renting. For rentals, you must respect return timelines and maintain the garment's condition. Fake orders or "wardrobing" (buying/renting to use once and return dishonestly) are strictly prohibited.

payments

4. Payments

All transactions are processed securely via Stripe. Relux Hub does not store your credit card information. Seller payouts are also handled via Stripe according to our standard processing schedule after order confirmation.

verified_user

5. Identity Verification

To ensure a safe marketplace, we require identity verification for all sellers. You must provide a clear photo of your CNIC (front and back) before your listings can go live. Your data is encrypted and handled according to our Privacy Policy.

Our platform is strictly for clothing and fashion accessories. No fake brands, inappropriate imagery, or non-apparel items are allowed. We reserve the right to remove any content that violates our community standards.

7. Account Safety

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your account credentials. Please report any suspicious activity to our support team immediately. Relux Hub is not liable for losses resulting from shared passwords.

8. Changes to Terms

We may update these terms periodically to reflect platform changes. While major updates will be communicated via email or app notifications, we encourage you to review this page occasionally.
