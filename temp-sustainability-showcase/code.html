Our Promise

"Circular fashion isn't just a trend; it's the future of how we inhabit the world."

At Relux Hub, we recognize that luxury carries a responsibility. The fashion industry is one of the most resource-intensive sectors globally. Our commitment is to offer a curated alternative where quality, craftsmanship, and sustainability coexist.

We partner with independent artisans and major fashion houses alike to verify the provenance of every item. From our carbon-neutral shipping routes to our biodegradable packaging, every touchpoint is designed with the planet in mind.

Helena de Vries

Director of Sustainability, Relux Hub
