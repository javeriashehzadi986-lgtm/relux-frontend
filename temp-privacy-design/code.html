Relux Hub Privacy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Relux Hub collects, uses, and protects your information as we build a sustainable fashion future together.

person_search

Information We Collect

We collect data to ensure a seamless circular fashion experience:

  • Name & Email
  • Phone & Profile Info
  • CNIC images (Identity verification)
  • Listing & Product details
settings_suggest

Usage & Purpose

Your data powers account management, seller verification, rental processing, and platform improvements to serve you better.

account_balance_wallet

Secure Payments

All transactions are securely processed via Stripe. Relux Hub does not store your full card information; we prioritize your financial safety through industry-leading encryption.

verified_user

Seller Verification

CNIC images are stored in a highly secure, encrypted vault and are never displayed publicly. They are strictly for trust-building within the community.

share_off

Information Sharing

We hold a strict policy: No selling to 3rd parties. Data is only shared for core operations or legal compliance.

Security Measures

While we use robust protection measures, users are responsible for keeping their credentials safe. Change your password periodically to stay secure.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or request the deletion of your account. Contact our support team for any data-related inquiries.

update

Policy Updates

We periodically update this policy to reflect platform changes. Major updates will be communicated via your registered email or in-app notifications.
